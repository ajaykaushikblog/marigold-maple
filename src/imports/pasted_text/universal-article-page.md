PHASE 3 — CREATE THE UNIVERSAL ARTICLE PAGE

The Homepage and Universal Category Page are already created.

Do NOT redesign either of them.

Keep the exact existing design system:
- typography
- colors
- spacing
- card styles
- image treatment
- category badges
- header
- footer
- newsletter
- advertisement styling
- responsive behavior

Now create the UNIVERSAL ARTICLE PAGE.

This must be a reusable article template for the entire website.

It must NOT be designed specifically for Weddings, Christmas, Recipes, Beauty, or any single category.

The same article template must support thousands of different articles.

==================================================
ARTICLE TYPES
==================================================

The article page must support:

- Standard Article
- Listicle / Ideas
- Guide
- Inspiration
- Seasonal Article
- Holiday Article
- Beauty Article
- Wedding Article
- Gift Guide
- Food Article
- Recipe-related article
- DIY-related article

Specialized Recipe and DIY pages will be created separately later.

==================================================
ARTICLE PAGE STRUCTURE
==================================================

Create this structure:

1. GLOBAL HEADER

Use the existing header from the homepage/category page.

Do not create a new visual header.

--------------------------------------------------

2. BREADCRUMBS

Example:

Home → Weddings → Wedding Decor → Article

The breadcrumb values must be dynamic.

--------------------------------------------------

3. ARTICLE HEADER

Include:

Category badge

Article title

Short excerpt/deck

Author information

Author name

Author photo

Published date

Updated date when applicable

Estimated reading time

Optional content type

Example:

WEDDING DECOR

25 Elegant Wedding Table Decoration Ideas

Beautiful table ideas for creating an elegant
and memorable wedding reception.

By Jane Smith
September 24, 2026
8 min read

Everything must be dynamic.

Do not hard-code these values.

--------------------------------------------------

4. FEATURED IMAGE

Large high-quality editorial image.

Support:

- responsive image
- image caption
- image credit
- alt text

The image should be optimized for an editorial/Pinterest-first website.

--------------------------------------------------

5. ARTICLE LAYOUT

Desktop:

Main article content on the left.

Sidebar on the right.

Use a comfortable reading width.

The sidebar should support:

Sticky advertisement

Optional newsletter

Optional related content

Mobile:

Single-column layout.

Hide the desktop sidebar.

Move advertisements into appropriate inline positions.

--------------------------------------------------

6. TABLE OF CONTENTS

Create an automatic-looking Table of Contents component.

It should support article headings such as:

Introduction

Ideas

Tips

How To

Frequently Asked Questions

Conclusion

The TOC should be optional.

Articles without enough headings should be able to hide it.

--------------------------------------------------

7. ARTICLE CONTENT BLOCK SYSTEM

Create reusable content blocks.

Support:

Paragraph

Heading H2

Heading H3

Image

Image Gallery

Image + Caption

Quote

Bullet List

Numbered List

Checklist

Callout

Tip Box

Info Box

Video Embed

Product/Affiliate Recommendation

Advertisement

Newsletter

Divider

Related Article

The design should make these blocks feel like one consistent editorial article.

--------------------------------------------------

8. PINTEREST-FIRST FEATURES

Include a Pinterest Save button.

The article must support:

Pinterest title

Pinterest description

Pinterest image

Pinterest-friendly vertical images

Do NOT make the entire website look like Pinterest.

Keep the premium editorial publication aesthetic.

--------------------------------------------------

9. SOCIAL SHARING

Create share controls for:

Pinterest

Facebook

X

Email

Copy Link

Keep them clean and unobtrusive.

--------------------------------------------------

10. ADVERTISEMENT SYSTEM

Create reusable advertisement placements:

Article top ad

Article inline ad

Article middle ad

Article bottom ad

Desktop sticky sidebar ad

Mobile inline ad

Advertisement areas must reserve visual space so advertisements do not cause major layout shifts.

Do not allow advertisements to overlap article content.

--------------------------------------------------

11. AUTHOR BIO

At the end of the article create:

Author photo

Author name

Short biography

Expertise

Optional social links

Link to author's profile

Example:

Written by Jane Smith

Jane is a lifestyle writer specializing in
weddings, celebrations and seasonal inspiration.

Everything must be dynamic.

--------------------------------------------------

12. RELATED ARTICLES

Create an automatic related-content section.

Display approximately 10 related articles.

Related articles can be selected using:

Same category

Same subcategory

Same occasion

Same season

Shared tags

Shared content type

The system should prioritize the most relevant relationships.

Also allow an administrator to manually override related articles later.

--------------------------------------------------

13. MORE INSPIRATION

After the primary related articles section, create another content section:

"More Inspiration"

This should provide additional relevant content.

It must be CMS-driven.

--------------------------------------------------

14. NEWSLETTER

Use the existing newsletter component/design.

Do not redesign it.

--------------------------------------------------

15. FOOTER

Use the existing footer.

Do not redesign it.
